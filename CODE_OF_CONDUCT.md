# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the PatchPilot community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and professional.
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
* Focusing on what is best for the community.
* Showing empathy towards other contributors.
* Helping new contributors learn and grow.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Spam, malicious code, or intentionally harmful contributions.
* Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

* GitHub repositories
* Issues and Pull Requests
* Discussions and community forums
* Project documentation
* Any official communication channels related to PatchPilot

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer.

Project maintainers will review and investigate all reports fairly and appropriately and will take any action deemed necessary to maintain a healthy community.
